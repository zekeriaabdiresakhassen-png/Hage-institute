About Us

Welcome to HAGE Institute

A dedicated online learning home for students who want to read, recite, memorize and understand the Qur'an.

HAGE Institute for Qur'an Al-Karim is based in Jijiga and provides Qur'an learning through an organized online learning environment.

Our aim is to encourage regular study and help learners build confidence in their Qur'an journey through structured learning.

  • Qur'an reading and recitation
  • Tajweed learning
  • Memorization support
  • Foundational Islamic learning

Our Learning Philosophy

Learning the Qur'an is a journey that requires patience, consistency and guidance. HAGE Institute aims to provide learners with a respectful and organized environment for that journey.
