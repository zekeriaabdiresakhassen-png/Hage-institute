About HAGE Institute

Based in Jijiga, HAGE Institute for Qur'an Al-Karim was established to bridge the gap between traditional Islamic education and modern virtual learning convenience.

Our mission is to empower Muslims worldwide with precise Qur'an recitation, thorough Tajweed mastery, and systematic memorization plans—all through patient, certified teachers and flexible online scheduling.
