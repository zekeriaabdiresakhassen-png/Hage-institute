About HAGE Institute

A simple online learning home for people who want to learn, read, recite and understand the Qur'an.

HAGE Institute for Qur'an Al-Karim is based in Jijiga and provides Qur'an learning online. The institute is focused on a respectful learning environment and clear, consistent study.

Whether you are beginning your Qur'an journey or strengthening your recitation, the institute is designed to support regular learning.

Our focus

Qur'an learning, recitation, Tajweed, memorization and foundational Islamic learning.
